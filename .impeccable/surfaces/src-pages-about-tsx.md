# Public pages

Mode: Read on About; Persuade on landing. Preserve the established Living Chronicle identity and factual origin, development status, links, and license. The user requests immersive imagery and rejects generic gradient panels. Scope: every public navigation destination (landing, About, How to Play, login, registration, news, leaderboards), news detail, and account recovery, plus operator attribution in the shared footer. Public How to Play, leaderboards, and news articles must remain accessible without a player session.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Enter a kingdom, then read its chronicle. Replace repeated icon tiles with a painted architectural scene and an asymmetric reading layout.

OWN-WORLD: Existing stone, antique gold, Chomsky titles, Inter body copy, race-aware tokens, heraldic shields, and the existing FantasyFrame corner treatment.

STORY: Learn the game's origin and status, identify its developer and LLC operator, then find news, support, or start playing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide throne-room painting, title and concise copy on its dark left side, detailed architecture on the right. Beneath it, a readable origin passage beside a narrow framed development-status ledger. Landing carries the same hero treatment with registration and login actions. Login, registration, and recovery share a painted entrance beside an inset form; news uses a dispatch opening and spacious article surfaces. How to Play and leaderboards use new war-table artwork, with continuous guide chapters and framed data ledgers respectively.

FORM: An illustrated chronicle extending the pinned Living Chronicle world; code-led implementation, no new-world seed. No scrolling or hover effect required to access content.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
